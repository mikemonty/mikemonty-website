## Overview

Memento was Mike’s 2020 aluminum yo-yo release. Mike named it after **memento mori**, the Stoic reminder of mortality. It is one of the best-documented yo-yos in the archive, with recovered specs and production notes.

## Specs

- **Diameter:** 56mm
- **Width:** 46mm
- **Weight:** 65.8g
- **Material:** 6061 aluminum
- **Axle:** 12mm T4
- **Gap:** 4.6mm
- **Original price:** $65

## Release notes

The first release came in **red, blue, and gray**, with gray selling out quickly. A larger second run followed in 2020 with six colors: **pink, purple, gray, orange, olive branch, and clear**. The second run reduced the gap by **0.2mm** and was scheduled for July 3, 2020 at 3pm Mountain.

## Story notes

A March 2020 journal note says Mike decided on the name Memento after Memento Mori from the Stoics. He also noted that the laser engraving looked amazing and that the yo-yo used a 12mm axle instead of the 14mm he expected.
