## Overview

The 3D Printable Cambium is a digital version of Mike’s handmade fixed axle Cambium. Mike had already printed and tested a Cambium in 2022, then uploaded the design to Thingiverse in 2024 so other yo-yoers could download and print one themselves.

## Known details

- **Based on:** Cambium
- **Type:** 3D-printable fixed axle yo-yo design
- **Printed/tested:** documented in 2022
- **Public upload:** Thingiverse upload documented April 30, 2024
- **Specs:** not recovered yet

## Story notes

A 2024 journal note says Mike uploaded the 3D printable Cambium design to Thingiverse and posted about it to Instagram. A few people downloaded it and said they would print it. The note also mentions a conversation with a Canadian yo-yoer about how 3D printing could let people avoid some of the friction around shipping physical yo-yos across borders.
