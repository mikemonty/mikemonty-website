## Overview

Roots was the first **Burgeon Yo-Yos** model: a handmade **fixed axle** wooden yo-yo. The name came from the idea of building a root system — the first thing Burgeon could make in-house, and the foundation for future growth.

## Known details

- **Brand:** Burgeon Yo-Yos
- **Type:** fixed axle wooden yo-yo
- **First documented:** January 2017 work, with notes and customer updates through spring/summer 2017
- **Later documented:** Roots-making work appears again in 2019
- **Specs:** not recovered yet

## Story notes

A May 2017 update says Mike and the Burgeon Yo-Yos project “started the ball rolling” in January, made the Roots fixed axle throw, and got a new Sprout prototype from the machinist. Life, a move, family emergencies, and a broken lathe interrupted production, but Roots orders, giveaways, samples, and custom woodburned versions were still part of the work queue.
