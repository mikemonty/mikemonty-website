## Overview

Tinder was a **Montgomery Yo-Yo Company** release. The strongest surviving reference is Mike’s August 2012 vlog, “Shipped the Tinders,” which says the Tinders had shipped to the people who preordered them.

## Known details

- **Era:** Montgomery Yo-Yo Company / MYYC
- **Documented shipment:** August 2012
- **Known media:** `2012-08-10: Shipped the Tinders`
- **Specs:** not recovered yet

## Story notes

Tinder is documented as a preorder product rather than only a concept. The surviving video description also places it beside Mike’s shirts preorder, a yo-yo class he was preparing to teach, and his how-to yo-yo book work.
