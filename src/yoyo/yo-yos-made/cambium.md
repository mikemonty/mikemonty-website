## Overview

Cambium was the first yo-yo developed under the **MikeMonty** brand. It was a handmade, one-piece wooden fixed axle yo-yo, named by Mike’s friend Ben.

## Known details

- **Brand:** MikeMonty
- **Type:** fixed axle wooden yo-yo
- **Construction:** one solid piece of wood
- **Woods documented:** Cherry and Walnut
- **Typical weight:** 36g–40g
- **Best suited for:** kickflips and stalls
- **First batch:** about nine made; several given away, the rest sold
- **First documented:** July 2019

## Story notes

Cambium came after Roots and marked a shift into MikeMonty-branded yo-yos. The first batch was tiny: about nine made, with some sold and some given away. A later giveaway template described Cambium as the only fixed axle yo-yo Mike was producing at the time.
