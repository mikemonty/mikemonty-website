## Overview

Forte was Mike’s **signature yo-yo with MonkeyfingeR Design**. It should be treated a little differently from the yo-yos Mike personally produced: Forte was a signature / collaboration model tied to Mike through sponsorship and promotion.

## Known details

- **Maker:** MonkeyfingeR Design
- **Role:** Mike Montgomery signature yo-yo
- **Documented date:** introduced in Mike’s 2013 video archive
- **Known use:** Forte Freestyles, 2013 Nationals footage, Boise trick video with Tyler Jorgensen
- **Specs:** not recovered yet

## Story notes

Forte became a regular on-camera throw for Mike. It appears in Let’s Play Yo-Yo, Forte Freestyles, a Boise video with Tyler Jorgensen at Time Zone Toys, and footage from the 2013 National Yo-Yo Contest where Mike had other players try his Forte on camera.
