## Overview

BassBoost was an early **Double-Take Industries** yo-yo associated with Mike Montgomery. The clearest surviving local record is a May 2010 trick video made because people had been asking to see the new Double-Take Industries BassBoost yo-yo in action.

## Known details

- **Era:** Double-Take Industries / early MikeMonty yo-yo era
- **Documented date:** May 3, 2010
- **Known media:** `BassBoost` trick video
- **Associated tags found locally:** BassBoost Yo-Yo, Double-Take Industries, Michael Montgomery, MikeMonty, Emmett, Idaho, Black Canyon
- **Specs:** not recovered yet

## Story notes

BassBoost belongs to the earliest public layer of Mike’s yo-yo-making history. The surviving video description frames it as a new yo-yo people wanted to see in action, and the clip doubled as Mike’s monthly trick video at the time.
