## Overview

Mori was a MikeMonty aluminum yo-yo release with its production run arriving in April 2021. It followed Memento in the MikeMonty aluminum timeline and remained part of Mike’s inventory and video work for years.

## Known details

- **Brand:** MikeMonty
- **Type:** aluminum yo-yo
- **Production arrival:** April 2021
- **Manufacturer noted locally:** MagicYoYo
- **Known colors/references:** Blackberry, purple, Sienna
- **Specs:** not recovered yet

## Story notes

A journal note from April 10, 2021 says the Moris arrived the previous afternoon. Mike named the dark color **Blackberry** because of how dark it turned out, mentioned MagicYoYo’s color handling, and began the testing, grading, and labeling work.

Mori later appears in trick videos, giveaways, eBay listings, and sales notes. A March 2023 journal note records Mike’s first yo-yo sale for crypto: someone bought a Mori with Ethereum for about $55 USD.
